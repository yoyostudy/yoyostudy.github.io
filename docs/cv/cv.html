RBC Borealis|Vancouver, Canada2026.01 – 2026.09
Mitacs Research Intern
  • Identified a state-supervision mismatch in looped models such as tiny recursive models (TRMs): with deep supervision and gradient detachment, early steps are trained on answers they cannot yet derive.
  • Designed adaptive supervision that trains each step only on what the model can already derive, improving Sudoku-Extreme accuracy by 7.9 points with up to 1.79× faster training and outperforming curriculum learning and diffusion-style supervision baselines.
  • Revealed that supervision shapes the recursive dynamics: under adaptive supervision the recursion settles and keeps its correct answers, so additional test-time iterations keep improving accuracy, whereas dense supervision leaves the recursion drifting and overwriting solved answers.
  • Resulted in a manuscript under review at a major machine learning conference.
